Malungelo continues to build momentum in the South African music scene, with several exciting projects lined up for fans. SneTech Media is preparing to launch a new podcast, adding another platform for conversations around music, entertainment and culture. The label has also continued releasing new music, including Malungelo's 2026 collaboration “Life With You” featuring Kaylow and Zanda Zakuza, released in February under SneTech Media..

Fans can also look forward to “Ithuba”, an upcoming collaboration featuring Malungelo alongside Nokwazi and Big Zulu, which is expected to arrive towards the end of September. The project adds to Malungelo's growing list of collaborations with established South African artists, following previous work with artists including Nokwazi, Xowla and Big Zulu..

Malungelo FC is also making headlines away from the recording studio, as the team continues its football campaign and looks to maintain its position in the Rand West League. Meanwhile, music fans have another collaboration to anticipate, with Tipcee and Malungelo reportedly working on a new project that is expected to bring together their distinctive sounds
