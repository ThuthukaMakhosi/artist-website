About

Malungelo

Background

Sinethemba Malungelo Nyawo, professionally known as Malungelo, is a South African entrepreneur, musician, and creative driven by excellence, innovation, and impact. He is the Director of Circle Rail, a Fochville- based engineering company providing services across South Africa and neighbouring countries, with a workforce of over 300 employees and partnerships with leading organisations in the mining sector.

Malungelo is also the founder of Snetech Media, established in 2021, specialising in video production, photography, event coverage, content creation, and artist development. His passion for music began at a young age, winning several gospel music competitions and later pursuing songwriting and performance. His music is inspired by everyday life and aims to inspire and connect with listeners.

Through the Malungelo Foundation, he also gives back to the community by supporting local initiatives, including soccer tournaments. Across business, music, and community development, Malungelo continues to build ventures that create opportunities and leave a lasting impact.

The Artist

Malungelo’s music is released under his own label, SneTech Media. A talented producer, composer, and singer, he has built his musical journey around creativity, collaboration, and a passion for developing new talent.

His standout hit, “Sne Mali,” has become one of the defining songs of his catalogue, further establishing his presence in the South African music scene. He has worked with acclaimed artists including Big Zulu, Nokwazi, Mduduzi Ncube, Zintle Kwaaiman, and Kaylow, and recently collaborated with Big Zulu and Nokwazi on “Ithuba.”

Keeping the momentum going, Malungelo recently released “Angesabi,” featuring Rethabile Khumalo, adding another exciting chapter to his growing musical catalogue. Beyond his own music, Malungelo mentors and collaborates with upcoming artists, helping them develop their sound and reach greater heights. Through SneTech Media, he continues to create, collaborate, and build a platform for the next generation of artists.

Achievements

MUSIC & INDUSTRY RECOGNITION.

AWARDS 2022 WWMRA Award | 2022 Eminent Music Video Award “Nguwe” featuring Mduduzi Ncube & Xowla | 2023 EVMA Artist of the Year.
RADIO & TV CHARTS Malungelo’s music has reached audiences across more than 48 radio stations, including Ukhozi FM, Lesedi FM, Motsweding FM and Metro FM. His hit single “Sne Mali” featuring Nokwazi has surpassed 1.5 million YouTube views, while “Till the Morning” featuring DJ Tira, Qwabe Twins and Joccy reached the Top 5 on radio and TV charts. His latest release, “Angesabi” featuring Rethabile Khumalo, has also entered the Top 5 on radio charts, further cementing his growing success in the music industry.
FOOTBALL Malungelo owns and supports a local soccer team that has won several tournaments and competes within the West Rand region, further reflecting his commitment to sport and community development.
