Background

The Malungelo Foundation is passionate about giving back and making a difference in communities across South Africa. The foundation regularly hosts local soccer tournaments and sporting events, bringing communities together while creating opportunities for young talent. It also supports schools in need through occasional donations, helping provide much-needed resources and support to learners.

The Malungelo Foundation continues to make a meaningful impact through sport and community development across South Africa. The foundation has hosted soccer tournaments in KwaZulu-Natal, North West and Gauteng, bringing together communities and attracting some of the country’s biggest sporting and entertainment names, including soccer legend Simphiwe Tshabalala and Cassper Nyovest. Beyond the football field, the foundation has taken its commitment to communities across KwaZulu-Natal and Gauteng, visiting schools in need to provide donations, improve facilities and help create better environments for learners. Through sport, education and community outreach, the foundation continues to use its platform to give back and create opportunities where they are needed most.
